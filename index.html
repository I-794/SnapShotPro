A full studio, no install.

Thirty-plus tools for framing, styling, annotating, and shipping screenshots, running entirely on your machine.

AI tools, on your terms

Remove backgrounds in-browser, pull text out with OCR, and generate alt text or captions with your own Claude or GPT key. Keys never leave the page.

Backgrounds worth keeping

Hand-tuned gradients, draggable mesh blobs, solid fills, or transparent. Extract a palette straight from your image.

App Store sets & batch

Design one mockup, then export a full set of captioned screenshots at every store size. Or run a template across many images and download the ZIP.

Device & window frames

macOS, Windows, iPhone, and browser chrome, plus laptop and phone mockup scenes.

Generative AI edits

Replace a background from a prompt, extend the canvas past its edges, or brush away anything you don't want.

Video, framed

Drop in a clip, trim it, set it inside any device, and export an MP4 or GIF.

Animation & GIF

Fade, slide, bounce, and scale presets. Export the result as an animated GIF.

Annotate & redact

Arrows, shapes, freehand, numbered callouts, stickers, blur redaction, and spotlight.

Share & export anywhere

One-click links, embed codes, and QR. Export presets sized for X, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, and TikTok, in PNG, JPEG, or WebP.

Installable & offline

Add it to your dock as a PWA. The editor runs offline; only cloud AI and sharing need a connection, and nothing uploads unless you ask.
