Every release

  • v16.2June 2026

    Pattern backgrounds and art filters

    • Pattern backgrounds. A new Pattern background joins gradient, mesh, and solid. Pick dots, a grid, lines, checker, or diagonal, then set the foreground and background colors, the tile size, the opacity, and an angle. It renders behind your shot like any other background and bakes into exports.
    • Art filter presets. One-tap looks sit above the filter sliders: Noir, Vintage, Vivid, and Faded, plus None to reset. Each sets brightness, contrast, saturation, and tone together in a single click, and the sliders move to match so you can fine-tune from there.
  • v16.1June 2026

    Liquid glass and film grain

    • Liquid glass. Drag a frosted glass panel over your screenshot with the new glass tool. It samples the pixels behind it and blurs them, so it really refracts your content rather than sitting as a flat tint, and the blur, tint, corner radius, and rim highlight are all adjustable. It bakes into every export.
    • Film grain. Lay a cinematic grain texture over the whole canvas, with controls for amount, grain size, blend mode, and a monochrome toggle. The texture sits under your text, watermark, and logo so they stay crisp, and it costs nothing while it is switched off.
    • Both bake into exports. Glass and grain render through the same path as everything else, so they appear in PNG, JPEG, batch, and App Store exports, ride along in saved projects and version history, and are fully undoable. Designs saved before v16 render unchanged.
  • v16.0June 2026

    Vector shape tools

    • A shape library. Five tools join the markup bar: line, ellipse, triangle, polygon, and star. Each draws live as you drag, and once placed it selects, moves, nudges, aligns, and deletes exactly like an arrow or a text label, because shapes reuse the same annotation plumbing.
    • Fill or stroke. Toggle fill on any closeable shape and pick a fill color, or leave it a clean outline. The setting bakes into each shape as you draw it, so one can be a solid badge while the next stays a hollow frame.
    • Adjustable polygons and stars. Choose how many sides a polygon has and how many points a star has, anywhere from three to twelve, for anything from a triangle callout to a twelve-point burst. Shapes bake into every export and ride along in saved projects and undo history.
  • v15.2June 2026

    Motion on stills

    • Ken Burns pan and zoom. Bring a still photo to life with a slow pan and zoom. Pick zoom in, zoom out, pan left, pan right, or a classic combined move, then press play to preview it. It rides the same clock as your animations, so it exports as motion too, and turns off automatically while a video clip is loaded.
    • Entrance animations for any element. The main image is no longer the only thing that can animate. Give a text overlay, an annotation, or an added image its own entrance, set from the layers panel, and each one plays on its own track so elements can animate independently of one another.
    • Easing curves. Every animation track now carries its own easing, picked from a library of eight curves: linear, ease in, ease out, ease in-out, a stronger ease in-out, overshoot, elastic, and bounce. Choose one per track so each element moves with its own character.
    • Animation saves and undoes. Animation now rides along in saved projects and version history and is fully undoable, just like the rest of your design. Loaded and shared designs always open paused at the start, never mid-playback.
    • Export a still as MP4. Animated stills and Ken Burns moves now export to MP4, not just GIF, with the same resolution and quality controls. MP4 uses your browser's built-in video encoder (Chrome and Edge today), and falls back to a clear message with GIF as the alternative where it is not available.
  • v15.1June 2026

    Timeline scrubber and export controls

    • Timeline scrubber. The two trim sliders are now a filmstrip timeline: drag the in and out handles to trim, drag the body to scrub, and watch a live playhead during playback. Everything snaps to whole frames, the labels read both seconds and frame number, and you can step a frame with the comma and period keys or set the in and out points with the bracket keys.
    • Motion export controls. Exporting a clip as MP4 or GIF now offers a resolution multiplier (0.5×, 1×, or 2× the canvas), a quality preset, and a GIF loop choice of loop forever or play once, so you can trade file size against sharpness without resizing the canvas.
  • v15.0June 2026

    Layer blending and opacity

    • Layer blend modes. Any layer can now composite with a blend mode instead of just stacking on top. Ten curated modes ship: normal, multiply, screen, overlay, soft light, hard light, color dodge, color burn, difference, and luminosity. Set one on the main screenshot, an annotation, an added image, or a text overlay, straight from the layers panel.
    • Per-layer opacity. Each layer has its own opacity slider, so you can fade a watermark, soften an overlay, or ghost an added image without changing anything else. Blend mode and opacity ride along in saved projects and version history, and both are undoable. Designs saved before v15 render unchanged.
  • v14June 2026

    Alignment, reflections, and text effects

    • Snapping and alignment guides. Dragging an element now snaps it to the canvas center and edges, and to other elements, with blue guide lines that show only while you drag. Nudge a selection with the arrow keys (hold Shift for 10px steps), or align it to the canvas from a compact toolbar. Guides never appear in an export.
    • Device reflection. Add a soft mirrored reflection beneath any device mockup or framed screenshot for the classic product-shot look. Opacity, length, and gap are adjustable, the reflection follows a tilted mockup, and it bakes into exports.
    • Richer text effects. Text overlays gained an outline, a gradient fill, a highlight box, and a drop shadow, usable on their own or together with the correct layering applied for you. Designs saved before v14 keep rendering unchanged.
  • v13.6June 2026

    More background gradients

    • Twelve new gradients. The Background tab now ships twenty preset gradients instead of eight. The additions cover warm corals and embers, cool lagoons and tides, fresh greens, soft blush and peach, and two darker fills for moodier shots.
    • Still fully editable. Each new preset drops into the existing gradient editor. Tweak the color stops, change the angle, or switch to radial, and the preset is just your starting point.
  • v13.5June 2026

    Studio redesign

    • A new workspace. The editor moved from one long scrolling sidebar to an icon rail plus a single contextual panel. Eight tabs (Import, Adjust, Background, Frame, Markup, AI, Export, Project) keep every tool one click away, and the studio remembers the tab you were on.
    • First-run welcome. A short, dismissible tour introduces the rail, multi-page decks, and autosave the first time you open the studio.
    • Same tools, calmer surface. Flatter accents and a sticky panel header make a deep toolset easier to scan. Nothing was removed, and on small screens the panels still collapse into one scroll.
  • v13June 2026

    Multi-page decks

    • Multi-page documents. A project can now hold many pages, each with its own canvas size and design. Add, duplicate, reorder, and delete them from a filmstrip beneath the canvas. Switching pages just saves the current one and loads the next, so the editor stays exactly as it was.
    • Deck export. Export the entire document as a multi-page PDF (via jsPDF) or a numbered set of images in a ZIP, rendered page by page to keep memory bounded.
    • Present mode. Play the whole deck full screen with arrow-key navigation, rendered straight from your pages. Pages and decks ride along inside the v12 project and version history.
  • v12June 2026

    Projects & version history

    • Project workspace. Every design is a named project with its own thumbnail. Open, rename, duplicate, or delete from one dashboard, and pick up exactly where you left off.
    • Version history. Automatic and named snapshots with a visual timeline: restore any point in time, or fork a version into a brand-new project.
    • Autosave, online or off. Work saves to your browser as you edit, then syncs across devices once you sign in. Designs are stored whole, artwork included.
  • v11June 2026

    Record, generate, collaborate

    • Screen recording + auto-zoom. Record your screen in the browser (getDisplayMedia + MediaRecorder); SnapShot-Pro eases in on every click, then you frame the clip in any device and export MP4 or GIF.
    • AI Art Director. Describe a vibe and the model returns a full design spec (background, gradient/mesh, device frame, headline, shadow), validated against existing presets and applied via the normal undoable path.
    • Live collaboration. Share a ?collab= link and design together in real time over Supabase Realtime, with live cursors and synced, history-safe edits.
    • Also in v11. Load media straight from a URL or YouTube link, export App Store sets localized into multiple languages, and publish or browse designs in a new community gallery.
  • v10.0May 2026

    Brand Kit

    • Save your brand once. Bundle your colors, heading font, signature gradient or mesh, device colorway, and a logo + text watermark into a named Brand Kit, stored right in your browser.
    • Apply in one click. Push the whole identity onto a single design, or onto an entire App Store set so every panel matches, captions and all.
    • Logo watermark. Drop in a logo and place it in any corner; it rides along on every exported screenshot and store panel.
  • v9.0 - 9.2May 2026

    Sets, generative AI, and video

    • App Store screenshot sets & batch. Design one mockup, then export a full set of captioned screenshots at every store size. Run a template across many images and download a ZIP.
    • Generative AI edits. Replace a background from a prompt, extend the canvas past its edges, and a magic eraser that removes objects and fills the gap.
    • Video clips. Drop in a clip, trim it, frame it inside any device mockup, and export an MP4 or GIF.
  • v8.1May 2026

    Deeper mockups

    • Baked 3D perspective into device mockups so tilt now carries through to the export, not just the preview.
    • Added Android (Pixel) and Windows laptop frames alongside the Apple lineup.

    • Procedural Apple-device frames drawn entirely on canvas: iPhone, iPad, MacBook, Apple Watch, and Studio Display, with recolorable finishes and accurate corners.
    • One-click hero presets that set device, background, shadow, and canvas size together.
