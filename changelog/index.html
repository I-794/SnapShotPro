Every release

  • v11June 2026

    Record, generate, collaborate

    • Screen recording + auto-zoom. Record your screen in the browser (getDisplayMedia + MediaRecorder); SnapShot-Pro eases in on every click, then you frame the clip in any device and export MP4 or GIF.
    • AI Art Director. Describe a vibe and the model returns a full design spec (background, gradient/mesh, device frame, headline, shadow), validated against existing presets and applied via the normal undoable path.
    • Live collaboration. Share a ?collab= link and design together in real time over Supabase Realtime, with live cursors and synced, history-safe edits.
    • Also in v11. Load media straight from a URL or YouTube link, export App Store sets localized into multiple languages, and publish or browse designs in a new community gallery.
  • v10.0May 2026

    Brand Kit

    • Save your brand once. Bundle your colors, heading font, signature gradient or mesh, device colorway, and a logo + text watermark into a named Brand Kit, stored right in your browser.
    • Apply in one click. Push the whole identity onto a single design, or onto an entire App Store set so every panel matches, captions and all.
    • Logo watermark. Drop in a logo and place it in any corner; it rides along on every exported screenshot and store panel.
  • v9.0 - 9.2May 2026

    Sets, generative AI, and video

    • App Store screenshot sets & batch. Design one mockup, then export a full set of captioned screenshots at every store size. Run a template across many images and download a ZIP.
    • Generative AI edits. Replace a background from a prompt, extend the canvas past its edges, and a magic eraser that removes objects and fills the gap.
    • Video clips. Drop in a clip, trim it, frame it inside any device mockup, and export an MP4 or GIF.
  • v8.1May 2026

    Deeper mockups

    • Baked 3D perspective into device mockups so tilt now carries through to the export, not just the preview.
    • Added Android (Pixel) and Windows laptop frames alongside the Apple lineup.
  • v8.0May 2026

    Mockup Studio

    • Procedural Apple-device frames drawn entirely on canvas: iPhone, iPad, MacBook, Apple Watch, and Studio Display, with recolorable finishes and accurate corners.
    • One-click hero presets that set device, background, shadow, and canvas size together.
  • v7.02026

    The visual overhaul

    • A full redesign with glassmorphism and micro-animations, plus element animation with GIF export.
    • AI auto-enhance, smart crop, and style transfer (watercolor, sketch, vintage, cyberpunk, noir).
    • Sharing built in: share links, embed codes, QR codes, and social export presets.
  • v4.0The beginning

    One file, one idea

    • SnapShot-Pro began as a single HTML file: drop in a screenshot, add a background and a frame, export. Everything since has built on that.
